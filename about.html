About Flappy Flopper

Welcome to Flappy Flopper, where the art of drawing contact meets the simple (yet infuriating) mechanics of classic tap-to-fly games.

Guide Sasha Vezenkov, master of the strategic stumble, through a gauntlet of reaching hands. Every tap is a tactical flop, a necessary maneuver to gain altitude and avoid the defense. How many fouls can *you* draw?

The Creator & Inspiration

This game was created by DimiInsights as a fun side-project and a humorous tribute to both Vezenkov's unique playing style and the iconic Flappy Bird game.

It's built with simple web technologies (HTML, CSS, and JavaScript) and countless hours of watching basketball highlights (for research, of course!).
