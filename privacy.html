Privacy Policy for Flappy Flopper

Effective Date: October 26, 2023

This Privacy Policy describes how Flappy Flopper ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our game and website.

Your privacy is important to us. This policy is intentionally simple because our game is simple.

Information We Collect

Flappy Flopper is designed to be played directly in your web browser. We do **not** collect any personally identifiable information (PII) from players.

  • We do not require account registration.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, location, or any other personal details.
  • We do not use third-party analytics that track your personal data across sites.

Cookies and Local Storage

The game may use your browser's **Local Storage** mechanism solely for the purpose of saving your high score or game progress directly on your own device.

This information is stored locally on your computer or device and is not transmitted to us or any third party. It does not contain any personal information.

You can typically clear your browser's local storage or cookies via your browser settings if you wish to remove this data.

Third-Party Links

Our website (specifically the About page) may contain links to other websites, such as Twitter. If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that site.

Note that these external sites are not operated by us. Therefore, we strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of these websites. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. Since we do not collect any such information, we are compliant with regulations like COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act).

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can find contact information on our About Page.
